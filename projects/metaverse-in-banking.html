Case Study · Emerging Tech · 2024

Metaverse in Banking

Designing a virtual bank branch concept for an immersive, avatar-based banking experience.

Role
Lead UX / Concept Designer
Timeline
6-week concept sprint
Platform
VR / WebXR
Team
1 designer · 1 3D artist · 1 technologist
◎
01 — Challenge

A virtual branch, but to what end?

The bank wanted a presence in the metaverse but had no framework for what a "virtual branch" should actually do. Early internal demos felt novel for a minute, then pointless — nobody could say why you'd come back.

02 — Research

Novelty wasn't the problem — trust was

Reviewed emerging XR banking pilots and ran moderated sessions with 9 users in an early VR prototype, watching how they reacted to the space and to avatar-led interactions.

01
People wanted the space to feel familiar, not sci-fi — recognizable banking cues mattered more than spectacle.
02
Trust dropped sharply when avatars felt too abstract for a conversation about their money.
03
Everyone wanted a fast, obvious way back to the normal app — the headset couldn't be the only door in.
03 — Process

From spatial sketch to tested prototype

Mapped the branch as a spatial flow before any 3D modeling, then built rough avatar-interaction prototypes to test where trust broke down.

04 — Solution

A hybrid branch, not a spectacle

Designed a simplified virtual space with recognizable banking landmarks — a teller desk, advisor pods — realistic-but-approachable avatars, and a one-tap exit back to the standard app at any point.
